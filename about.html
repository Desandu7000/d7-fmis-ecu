About the FMIS

What the system is for.

The Facilities Management Information System (FMIS) is the University’s web-based system for tracking moveable furniture assets across its campus buildings, managing room occupancy, coordinating office relocations and handling maintenance calls.

Every furniture item is assigned a GTIN-compliant EAN-13 barcode. Staff contact details are read from the University HR system. The actual furniture move is carried out by an external removalist firm; the FMIS does not handle payments to that firm.

Any authenticated staff member can view building plans and manage their own move requests and maintenance calls. Facilities Management Office (FMO) staff can also record and update furniture and building data, schedule moves and generate the FMO Asset Report.
