Facilities Management Information System

Internal system for the Edith Cowan University Facilities Management Office.

About this system

The FMIS helps the University keep track of moveable furniture assets across campus buildings. Staff use it to see building plans, request an office move, change or delete a move request, and log a maintenance call.

Facilities Management Office (FMO) staff can also record and update furniture assets, schedule and re-schedule moves, and generate an FMO asset report that lists the occupants and furniture in each room of a floor.

Staff contact details are read from the University HR system. The actual furniture move is carried out by an external removalist firm.
