New updates

Recent changes and announcements about the FMIS.

4 September 2026 – Maps area added

You can now view building floor plans, floor areas and the linked AutoCAD drawings from the new Maps area.

28 August 2026 – Furniture labels

The Update furniture asset screen can now print a GTIN-compliant EAN-13 label.

21 August 2026 – Maintenance calls

Staff can log a maintenance call for an office to be opened, lighting to be replaced or a carpet to be cleaned, and cancel a call while it is still Logged.
