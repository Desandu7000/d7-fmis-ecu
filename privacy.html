Privacy & cookie policy

How the FMIS handles personal information and cookies.

About this notice

The Facilities Management Information System (FMIS) is an internal system used by Kingsford University staff to manage furniture assets, room occupancy, office relocations and maintenance calls. This notice explains what information the FMIS collects and how it is used.

This site is a coursework prototype built for the CSI1241 Systems Analysis assignment. It has no server or database, so nothing entered on these pages is transmitted or stored. The policy below describes how the real system is intended to behave.

Information the FMIS uses

  • Staff identity and contact details – name and staff ID, read from the University HR system. The FMIS does not maintain its own copy of staff contact records.
  • Room occupancy – which staff member occupies which room.
  • Records you create – furniture assets, move requests and maintenance calls, including their status history, kept so the University can audit its moveable assets over time.

How the information is used

Personal information is used only to operate the FMIS: to identify the person who raised a request, to notify staff about changes to their relocation, and to produce the FMO Asset Report. It is not used for marketing and is not shared outside the University, except with the external removalist firm engaged to carry out a move.

Cookies

The live FMIS would use a single strictly-necessary session cookie to keep you signed in while you move between pages. No advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies are used. Because this prototype has no server, it sets no cookies at all.

Retention and access

Asset, move and maintenance records are retained for as long as the University needs them for asset management and auditing. Access is role-based: general staff can see their own requests, while Facilities Management Office staff can see and manage all records. Staff may ask the FMO to correct information held about them.

Contact

Questions about this notice should be directed to the Kingsford University Facilities Management Office.
